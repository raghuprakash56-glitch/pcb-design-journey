# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The PCB Design Journey project is committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and respectful environment for all members of our community, regardless of background, experience level, or identity.

## Our Standards

We pledge to interact with professionalism and respect. Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- **Respectful Communication:** Using inclusive language and treating others with dignity
- **Constructive Feedback:** Offering helpful criticism in a professional manner
- **Collaboration:** Working together to achieve shared learning goals
- **Inclusivity:** Welcoming people from diverse backgrounds and perspectives
- **Accountability:** Taking responsibility for our words and actions

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are considered unacceptable and will not be tolerated:

- Harassment, discrimination, or bullying of any kind
- Offensive language or personal attacks
- Sharing private information without consent
- Intentional disruption of discussions or community
- Any form of discriminatory behavior based on protected characteristics

## Reporting Violations

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior:

1. **Document the incident** with relevant details and context
2. **Report to project maintainers** via email or private message
3. **Provide details** to help us understand and address the issue

All reports will be handled confidentially and with appropriate discretion.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for enforcing these standards. Responses to violations may include:

- Warning or request for behavior modification
- Temporary suspension of community participation
- Permanent removal from the project community

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- All project spaces (issues, discussions, pull requests)
- Community events and gatherings
- Official communications
- Any space representing the project

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).

## Questions?

If you have questions about this Code of Conduct or need to report a concern, please contact the project maintainers.

Thank you for helping us maintain a positive and inclusive community! 🌟
